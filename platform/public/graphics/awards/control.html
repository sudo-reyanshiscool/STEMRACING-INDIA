Space or → next · ← back · a clicker's page keys work too · on the big screen press F for fullscreen. Click any line to jump to it.

Pick the nominees (two or three) and the winner for each award before the show. The winner list only offers that award's nominees. The screen will not move on to nominees or a winner that have not been picked. Picks are saved on this computer. Hide results swaps the team lists for “in” or “not in yet”, so you can run the show without seeing who won; each result still appears on the screen when you reach it.
