On screen: Next:

Space or → next · ← back · H holding screen · a clicker's page keys work too · on the big screen press F for fullscreen.

Everything shows in the one big-screen window. The holding screen is always running underneath, so there is something on screen at every moment; switching between Opening, Racing, Analysis and Awards here does not change the screen until you press a button.

Files from the platform

Drop the platform’s JSON downloads anywhere on this page: Heats (teams, race order, knockout winners, fastest car), Nominations (nominees), Awards (winners), Results (the podium) and Cars (sizes from scrutineering, for the analysis). Dropping a newer one updates what it covers.

Opening ceremony

Runs top to bottom with Next. Every team gets a walk-on card, in team-number order. The lines themselves are in opening.js. Click any line to jump to it.
